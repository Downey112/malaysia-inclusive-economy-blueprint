📊 Malaysia Inclusive Economy Blueprint — Interactive Policy Simulator
LINK: https://Downey112.github.io/malaysia-inclusive-economy-blueprint/

An interactive, single-page economic policy simulator and data visualization dashboard modeling the heterodox policy architecture of Dr. Muhammed Abdul Khalid.

This portfolio project translates complex econometric research, Distributional National Accounts (DINA) data, graduate hiring discrimination field audits, and urban PPR poverty studies into an explorable, interactive policy engine.

✨ Key Features & Interactive Modules

🏛️ Progressive Fiscal Feasibility Engine

Interactive sliders to model dynamic revenue generation from Capital Gains Tax (CGT), Inheritance Tax on multi-million estates, and high public pension taxes versus a 6% Goods and Services Tax (GST).

💼 Minimum Wage Staircase & Fair Hiring Roadmap

Dynamic scenario calculator projecting mandatory annual minimum wage step-ups toward a RM3,000 living wage baseline, integrated with private sector anti-discrimination policy insights.

👶 PPR "Cash Plus" Urban Safety Net Planner

Modular budget simulator quantifying coverage and required funding for early childhood nutritional support and direct cash assistance across DBKL, regional, and nationwide low-cost PPR housing schemes.

📊 13th Malaysia Plan (13MP) Benchmark Scorecard

Side-by-side comparison matrix contrasting official government targets with OECD-aligned heterodox benchmarks across poverty reduction, income equality (Gini ratio), and affordable housing delivery.

🛠️ Tech Stack & Architecture

Frontend Framework: Vanilla HTML5, JavaScript (ES6+)

Styling & Layout: Tailwind CSS (via CDN)

Data Visualizations: Chart.js (Canvas-based responsive charts)

Design System: Warm Institutional Palette (Sandstone, Deep Slate #1E293B, Warm Rust #9A3412, Progressive Emerald #059669)

Architecture: Single-Page Application (SPA), zero build tools required, responsive across all mobile & desktop viewports.

This application synthesizes research and empirical datasets from:

Lee, H. A., & Khalid, M. A. (2016). Discrimination of High Degree: Race and Earnings in a Match-Experiment among Malaysian Graduates.

Khalid, M. A., & Yang, L. (2019/2020). Distributional National Accounts (DINA) for Malaysia (World Inequality Lab).

UNICEF / UNFPA / DM Analytics (2023–2024). Living on the Edge / Families on the Edge longitudinal PPR housing studies.
