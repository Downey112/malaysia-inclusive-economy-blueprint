An interactive, single-page economic policy simulator and data visualization dashboard modeling the heterodox policy architecture of **Dr. Muhammed Abdul Khalid**.

This project translates complex econometric research, Distributional National Accounts (DINA) datasets, graduate hiring discrimination field audits, and urban PPR poverty studies into an explorable, client-side policy simulation engine.

🔗 **Live Application:** [Downey112.github.io/malaysia-inclusive-economy-blueprint](https://Downey112.github.io/malaysia-inclusive-economy-blueprint/)

---

## ✨ Key Features & Interactive Modules

### 🏛️ Progressive Fiscal Feasibility Engine
Interactive sliders model dynamic revenue generation from Capital Gains Tax (CGT), Inheritance Tax on multi-million ringgit estates, and progressive high public pension brackets versus a 6% Goods and Services Tax (GST).

### 💼 Minimum Wage Staircase & Fair Hiring Roadmap
Dynamic scenario calculator projecting mandatory annual minimum wage step-ups toward a RM3,000 living wage baseline, integrated with empirical private sector anti-discrimination policy insights.

### 👶 PPR "Cash Plus" Urban Safety Net Planner
Modular budget simulator quantifying coverage and required funding for early childhood nutritional support and direct cash assistance across DBKL, regional, and nationwide low-cost PPR housing schemes.

### 📊 13th Malaysia Plan (13MP) Benchmark Scorecard
Side-by-side comparison matrix contrasting official government targets with OECD-aligned heterodox benchmarks across poverty reduction, income equality (Gini ratio), and affordable housing delivery.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Architecture

* **Frontend Framework:** Vanilla HTML5, Modern JavaScript (ES6+)
* **Styling & Layout:** Tailwind CSS (via CDN)
* **Data Visualizations:** Chart.js (Canvas-based responsive charts)
* **Design System:** Warm Institutional Palette (Sandstone, Deep Slate `#1E293B`, Warm Rust `#9A3412`, Progressive Emerald `#059669`)
* **Architecture:** Zero-build Single-Page Application (SPA) with zero external server dependencies; fully responsive across mobile & desktop viewports.

## 📖 Research & Data Sources

This application synthesizes empirical research and macro datasets from:
1. **Khalid, M. A. (2014).** *The Colour of Inequality: Ethnicity, Class, and Income in Malaysia.*
2. **Lee, H. A., & Khalid, M. A. (2016).** *Discrimination of High Degree: Race and Earnings in a Match-Experiment among Malaysian Graduates.*
3. **Khalid, M. A., & Yang, L. (2019/2020).** *Distributional National Accounts (DINA) for Malaysia* (World Inequality Lab).
4. **UNICEF / UNFPA / DM Analytics (2023–2024).** *Living on the Edge / Families on the Edge* longitudinal PPR housing studies.
